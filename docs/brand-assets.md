# BITS Kurnool branding assets

- Institution identity and official web reference: [Brindavan Institute of Technology & Science](https://www.bitskurnool.edu.in/).
- The crest used by this app was cropped from an image result sourced from the official BITS Kurnool website. The original downloaded reference is retained at `apps/web/public/bits-official-banner.jpg`; the small seal crop is `apps/web/public/bits-seal.jpg`.
- The application uses the name **BITS Kurnool**, the campus location **NH-7, Peddatekur, Kurnool – 518218, Andhra Pradesh, India**, and fictional sample accounts only.
- The demo portal is an independent student-development project; its sample names, marks, and academic events are not official employee/student records.
